Philosophical Vision & Foundations

"If the odds are against you, you should still do it if the goal is important enough."

Born in Pretoria, South Africa, my journey began with self-taught computer programming at age 12, creating the space game Blastar. My pursuit of physics and economics at the University of Pennsylvania (Wharton School) reinforced a lifelong commitment to understanding how the universe works and solving existential risks facing humanity.

Rather than analyzing trendlines, I apply First-Principles Thinking: boiling problems down to their fundamental truths and reasoning up from there. Whether reducing the cost per kilogram to orbit by 10x or digitizing neural interface bandwidth, physics is the ultimate baseline limit.
